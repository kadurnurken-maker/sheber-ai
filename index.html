Tradition meets Code.

We are creating a digital eye for wrestlers. Sheber AI uses computer vision to track movements on the tatami, providing instant feedback like a professional coach.

Our project aims to make training safer and more accessible for every village and school in Kazakhstan.

The Engineering.

STEP 01

Capture

A simple smartphone camera records the throw from a side view.

STEP 02

Analyze

MediaPipe extracts 33 key skeleton points in real-time 3D space.

STEP 03

Evaluate

Python algorithms calculate joint angles and compare them to the gold standard.

STEP 04

Feedback

If the hip or back angle is off, the system alerts the wrestler instantly.
